#### Practical Improvements for Next Week

1. **Speed of Service:** Implement a system to manage customer orders more efficiently, such as using a queue system or a digital ordering system. This will help reduce wait times and improve the overall customer experience.

2. **Cleanliness:** Increase the frequency of cleaning tables and other common areas. This could be done by hiring additional staff or by training current staff to clean more frequently. Regularly scheduled cleaning checks can also be implemented to ensure that areas are kept clean.

3. **Pricing:** Consider offering discounts or promotions to attract more customers. Alternatively, provide a clear and transparent explanation of pricing to help customers understand the value they are getting for their money.

By focusing on these areas, the cafe can address the most common complaints and improve the overall customer experience.
