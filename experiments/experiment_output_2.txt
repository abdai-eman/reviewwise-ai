WEEKLY FEEDBACK REPORT

1. OVERALL SUMMARY
The cafe received a mix of positive and negative feedback this week. Customers appreciated the coffee quality and friendly staff, but also noted slow service, incorrect orders, dirty tables, and high prices. The cafe needs to address these issues to improve customer satisfaction.

2. MAIN POSITIVE FEEDBACK
- Coffee Quality: Customers praised the quality of the coffee, indicating that it was a strong point of the cafe.
- Service: Friendly and helpful staff were noted as positive aspects.
- Atmosphere: Customers appreciated the ambiance of the cafe.

4. THREE PRACTICAL IMPROVEMENTS
1. **Speed Up Service:** Implement a system to manage customer orders more efficiently, such as using a queue system or a digital ordering system to reduce wait times. This can be done by training staff to manage orders more quickly and by possibly investing in a more efficient point-of-sale system.
2. **Quality Control for Orders:** Establish a quality control process for orders to ensure accuracy and prompt correction of any mistakes. This could involve cross-checking orders with a supervisor or using a system that alerts staff to potential errors.
3. **Regular Cleaning of Tables:** Schedule regular cleaning of tables to maintain a clean and hygienic environment. This can be done by having a dedicated cleaning team or by training staff to clean tables more frequently during their breaks.
