WEEKLY FEEDBACK REPORT

1. OVERALL SUMMARY
This week, customers provided a mix of positive and negative feedback. The cafe received praise for its atmosphere, staff, and coffee quality, but also faced issues with service speed, cleanliness, and pricing. The cafe's atmosphere and staff were consistently appreciated, while service speed and cleanliness were the most frequently mentioned issues.

2. MAIN POSITIVE FEEDBACK
- Atmosphere and Staff: Customers appreciated the cafe's atmosphere and the friendly staff.
- Coffee Quality: The quality of the coffee was consistently praised.
- Pastries: The pastries were noted as delicious.

3. CUSTOMER COMPLAINTS
- Service Speed: Customers mentioned that service was slow in 4 reviews (Reviews 1, 8, 9, and 10).
- Cleanliness: Customers noted that tables were dirty in 2 reviews (Reviews 4 and 5).
- Pricing: Customers complained about high prices in 2 reviews (Reviews 6 and 7).

4. THREE PRACTICAL IMPROVEMENTS
1. Address the issue of slow service by training the staff to manage orders more efficiently and possibly implementing a system to alert staff when orders are behind schedule.
2. Increase the frequency of cleaning tables and restocking supplies to ensure they are always clean and in good condition.
3. Review and potentially adjust the pricing strategy to ensure it aligns with customer expectations and remains competitive in the market.
