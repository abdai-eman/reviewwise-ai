4. THREE PRACTICAL IMPROVEMENTS

1. **Enhance Service Speed**: Implement a system to track and manage customer orders more efficiently. Consider training the staff to better anticipate and manage customer orders, and possibly introduce a system to alert staff when a customer is waiting for a long time. This will help reduce the perceived wait time and improve customer satisfaction.

2. **Increase Staff Training on Table Cleaning**: Ensure that all staff members are trained to clean tables promptly and regularly. This could involve setting up a schedule for table cleaning or providing additional training on the importance of maintaining a clean environment. This will help address the issue of dirty tables and improve the overall cleanliness of the cafe.

3. **Improve Communication with Customers**: Develop a clear and consistent communication protocol for staff to use when explaining wait times or service issues. This could include a standardized script for staff to use when a customer expresses dissatisfaction with the service speed. Additionally, consider implementing a feedback system where customers can report issues directly to management, which can then be addressed promptly.

By focusing on these areas, we can address the main complaints and improve the overall customer experience at the cafe.
