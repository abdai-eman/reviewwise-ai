WEEKLY FEEDBACK REPORT

1. OVERALL SUMMARY

This week, we received 10 customer reviews. The overall sentiment is positive with a few areas needing improvement. The main positive feedback includes the quality of the coffee, friendly staff, and great atmosphere. The main complaints include slow service, cold coffee, long wait times, dirty tables, high prices, and loud music.

4. THREE PRACTICAL IMPROVEMENTS

1. **Increase Staff During Peak Hours:** Given that slow service was a recurring issue, especially in Review 1 and Review 8, it would be beneficial to increase the number of staff during peak hours to ensure faster service and reduce wait times.

2. **Regularly Check and Maintain Tables:** The cleanliness of the tables is crucial for a positive customer experience. Regularly checking and maintaining the cleanliness of the tables can help prevent issues like the ones mentioned in Review 5.

3. **Adjust Pricing Strategy:** Review 6 highlighted that prices were too high. Considering adjusting the pricing strategy to make the menu more affordable without compromising the quality of the products, could help attract more customers and improve customer satisfaction.

By implementing these improvements, we can enhance the overall customer experience and address the main issues identified in the feedback.
